What we collect

When you make an enquiry (through our website form, email, phone or WhatsApp): your name, email address, phone number, the type of event, date, location, approximate guest numbers, budget range if you share it, and anything else you tell us in your message.

When you book with us: the details we need to plan and run your event, such as billing details, venue and supplier information, schedules, guest numbers and, where relevant, dietary or accessibility requirements you choose to share. Dietary or health information is only used to plan your event, and only with your agreement.

When you use our website: technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, and pages visited. With your consent we use analytics cookies (see Cookies below).

When you contact us on social media: your profile name and the messages you send us.

How we use it and our lawful basis

  • Replying to your enquiry and preparing a proposal. Lawful basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract.
  • Planning and delivering your event, invoicing and taking payment. Lawful basis: performance of our contract with you.
  • Keeping financial records. Lawful basis: legal obligation.
  • Website analytics to understand how people use our site. Lawful basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Sending occasional news or offers by email. Only if you have opted in, and you can unsubscribe at any time. Lawful basis: consent.
  • Using photos from your event on our website or social media. Only with your permission, as agreed in our client contract.
  • Keeping our website secure and preventing spam. Lawful basis: our legitimate interests.

We never sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We only share what’s needed, with:

  • Suppliers and venues working on your event (for example caterers, florists, photographers), so they can deliver their part;
  • Service providers who run our business systems, such as our email provider (Google Workspace), website host (Hostinger), Google Analytics (with your consent), accounting software and WhatsApp (Meta) if you message us there;
  • Professional advisers such as our accountant, and authorities where the law requires it.

Some of these providers may store data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on safeguards approved under UK law, such as the UK adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Agreement/Addendum.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don’t lead to a booking: up to 12 months after our last contact.
  • Client and booking records: 6 years after the event, to meet tax and accounting rules.
  • Analytics data: up to 14 months (Google Analytics retention setting).
  • Marketing consent: until you unsubscribe.

Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies so it works properly. With your consent, we also use analytics cookies (Google Analytics) to see which pages are useful. You can accept or reject non-essential cookies in the cookie banner on any page of this site. Rejecting them won’t affect how the site works.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of your personal information;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • object to or restrict how we use it;
  • ask us to transfer it to you or another organisation;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

Email info@ovents.co.uk to use any of these rights. We’ll reply within one month.

Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113.
